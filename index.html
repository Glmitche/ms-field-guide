Your story
Four bridge themes — connect every payments concept back to your experience
"I bring something rare to this role: deep experience managing enterprise relationships and driving measurable outcomes, combined with genuine technology and product fluency. Most RMs can own a client relationship. Fewer can also help a client think through integrated payments strategy, diagnose conversion friction, or evaluate how their payment infrastructure connects to their broader technology stack."
— Your core positioning statement
Relationship management
At Reflekt Me, I was the primary point of contact for enterprise retail clients — managing onboarding, performance, escalations, and ongoing optimization against defined SLAs.
At FSU, I managed relationships across academic departments and external partners — coordinating stakeholders with competing priorities toward shared institutional goals. That internal navigation complexity is exactly what this role demands.
At SNHU, I served as liaison between platforms serving 280,000+ users and executive stakeholders — translating performance data into strategic recommendations and facilitating executive reviews.
Revenue & growth accountability
At Reflekt Me, our platform drove a 75% conversion rate for enterprise clients — a direct revenue outcome tied to client renewal and expansion decisions. I was accountable for client performance metrics, not just delivery.
At FSU, I oversaw $2M+ in combined budgets, ensuring resource allocation was tied to measurable program outcomes. Financial discipline in relationship management is not new to me.
My QBR experience was exactly re-contracting in practice: making the case for continued and expanded investment with data, analysis, and strategic framing.
Technology & product fluency
Integrated payments is where the market is moving, and my product background lets me have that conversation with clients in a way most RMs cannot. I understand APIs, integration complexity, and translating technical capability into business value.
At Reflekt Me, I managed platform integrations with enterprise retail clients — understanding the client's tech stack and how our solution fit into it was essential. That's the same conversation an RM has with a client about payment gateway integration.
At Fathom, I worked on a platform integrating AI, CRM, and workforce tools — I understand where integration friction points live and how to diagnose them.
Consultative, data-driven approach
At SNHU, I built reporting frameworks and dashboards to track engagement and operational performance — then used those insights to drive executive-level decisions. That's the QBR skill set.
At Reflekt Me, we led with conversion data and business impact — not product features. That's consultative selling, and it's the posture this role requires.
At FSU, early alert systems identified at-risk students before outcomes deteriorated. Same pattern in payments: monitor authorization rates, identify decline trends, intervene before the client feels the pain.
